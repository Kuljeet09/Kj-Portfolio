About Me

Turning Data into Business Decisions

I'm Kuljeet Kumar Singh, an MIS and Data Analytics professional based in Jharkhand. With an MBA and 3+ years of hands-on experience, I specialise in MIS reporting, dashboard creation, and automating routine reporting workflows.

At Horizon Group (Horizon Kia / Horizon Honda / Horizon Trucking), I handle daily, weekly, and monthly MIS reporting for Sales, Service, Finance, and Operations. I design interactive dashboards in Google Sheets, MS Excel, and Looker Studio, integrating data from FMS, IMS, and PMS systems into unified management views.

My automation work — using Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, and standardised templates — has reduced manual reporting effort by 35–40%, ensuring accurate, timely business intelligence for senior management.
